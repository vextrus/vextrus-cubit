VEXTRUS CUBIT — the vendored icon set: licence and provenance
=============================================================

What is in this directory
-------------------------
`glyphs.tsx` holds 34 SVG React components. They are HAND-WRITTEN source in this repository. No
icon package is installed, imported, fetched or bundled to produce them: `lucide-react` is NOT a
dependency of this tree (AM-08 PART 1 records it as not installed) and adding one is refused by
B-24 — the product carries its assets as source, and fetches nothing at build or at run time.

Why Lucide is named at all
--------------------------
Design Direction 00 §1 ("Iconography") binds the product to "one vendored stroke set (Lucide,
1.5 px, 16/20)". What is adopted from Lucide is its DRAWING SYSTEM — a 24-unit box, a uniform
1.5 px `currentColor` stroke, round caps and joins, no fill, and the shape vocabulary a reader of
CAD software already recognises. The geometry below was traced by hand against that system: each
path was restated from the shape's description, at the coordinates chosen here, and none of the
upstream path data was copied out of a package or a file. Where a glyph is a plain geometric
primitive (a plus, a cross, a chevron, a circle, a 3 × 3 grid) there is no authorship to carry in
either direction — the shape is the shape.

The taka sign
-------------
`IconTaka` is not an adopted icon: it is the Bengali taka sign U+09F3 traced as geometry, at this
set's stroke and box, because the vendored Bengali font subset that Design Direction 00 §5 asks for
could not be produced offline (no fontTools/pyftsubset and no Noto Sans Bengali on the machine, and
`pnpm exec` has no network). It is a stand-in with a deliberate exit: when a real Noto Sans Bengali
subset is vendored under `src/ui/fonts/` with its SIL OFL 1.1 text beside it, `MoneyText` stops
reading this component and this component is deleted. The Interpretation is recorded in
`src/ui/primitives/core/money-text.tsx`.
