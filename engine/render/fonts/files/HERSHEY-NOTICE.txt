rowmans.jhf: the Hershey Roman Simplex font (ASCII order, 96 glyphs, space to the degree circle)

Taken unmodified from https://github.com/kamalmostafa/hershey-fonts (commit
1356bf2f83d380fcef68c887e88675eb9d445d86, hershey-fonts/rowmans.jhf), whose data came from
http://emergent.unpythonic.net/software/hershey. The Hershey font data carries this notice, which
must be distributed with it:

			any other format *EXCEPT* the format distributed by

			the coordinate values as ASCII numbers.
