# The walk the owner shows

Every task serves one of these steps. The walker checks them against the newest `sprint` and writes
the result to WALK.md. The owner may edit this file; builders don't.

**What is shown is labelled as what it is.** If a step is not read yet, the screen says it is an
allowance or "not read yet"; it never shows an invented figure (ADR 0002).

## A. Drawings in (M0's finish line): must work
1. `bash docs/sprint/demo.sh` brings the product up from a fresh checkout in under 5 minutes.
2. The MD signs in, sees their Projects, and opens the demo Project (Site, Building).
3. The MD invites a QS; the QS signs in and sees only that Project.
4. The QS uploads the Sample Project's structural and architectural DWGs and the Edison set.
5. Each file reads. A file that cannot be read shows its Failed or Held row, in words a QS
   understands, with its next step.
6. The Drawing Set lists every sheet with a proposed number, title, Discipline, sheet type and
   storeys, each with its source.
7. Any sheet opens in the viewer, legible, with pan and zoom; the views within it are outlined.
8. The QS corrects one proposal, answers a Question, and confirms the sheet list (Takeoff Step 1).

## B. The frame, priced (an M1 slice, on the Sample Project)
9. The QS confirms the storeys and levels, then the grid.
10. Columns, beams and slabs are read as Elements, one column per storey, and the QS confirms them.
11. The frame shows in 3D; clicking an Element shows its Attributes.
12. The Priced BOQ shows concrete and formwork, priced by Rate Analyses on the starter Market Prices
    (PWD SoR 2022, Dhaka), in ৳; the Material Schedule shows cement, sand and aggregate in their
    units.
13. Every figure opens its Trace, down to the sheet it came from.
14. Steps not yet confirmed are marked allowances, so the MD sees a whole-building figure with its
    measured share.

## C. A glimpse of the showcase (an M2 slice)
15. The MD's Project Summary: the whole-building figure, the measured share, the Target Cost.
16. The Priced BOQ exports to Excel.
17. The 3D share link opens the frame for someone without an account.
