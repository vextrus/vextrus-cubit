# The MVP is priced per project by gross floor area; a firm subscription comes later

A Developer pays per project, priced in ৳ per sft of gross floor area, for the Takeoff, the Priced
BOQ, the Material Schedule and the Building Model of one building. A firm subscription arrives later,
once something lives across projects: the Developer's Rate Analyses and Market Prices, its Rule Set,
project memory, and cost control. For their first Takeoff, the first clients get our engineers
sitting with their QS ("done with you"). It earns revenue before the product is fully self-serve,
shows us where the Takeoff is slow, and protects the first impression.

Why:
- The Developer buys a result (what the building will cost), not a tool. Per-project pricing scales
  with building size, and Dhaka Developers dislike Western-style yearly subscriptions.
- The owner's market facts:
  - Most Developers estimate in-house, in a costing department.
  - A building's estimate costs roughly ৳1 lakh when bought, and may be more or much less, since
    Developers bargain hard.
  - In-house, the real cost is salaries and weeks of slow work.

  Vextrus's first value is cutting that time sharply, and that is the lever in the bargain.

Considered: per seat per year (rejected: disliked in the culture, and it prices the tool, not the
result).

## The price (owner's decision)
- **List price:** ৳4 per sft of gross floor area, with a minimum of ৳60,000 per building. A typical
  25,000 sft G+9 building comes to about ৳1 lakh, today's price for an estimate, delivered in days
  and with a 3D model and a Material Schedule on top.
- **Revisions:** revised drawings of the same building are included for 6 months.
- **Founding clients:** the first three Developers pay half, in exchange for:
  1. permission to use their Drawing Sets, locally, as Independent Sets (ADR 0005);
  2. a feedback session after each Takeoff;
  3. a reference if they are satisfied.
