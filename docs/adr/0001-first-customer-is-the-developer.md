# The first customer is the real-estate Developer

Vextrus's first paying customer is the Bangladeshi real-estate Developer: its MD buys, and its QS uses
the product daily. We chose it over tender contractors (PWD/LGED contractors usually receive the BOQ
and only price it), design consultants (thin fees, little use for downstream modules) and freelance
QS firms (low willingness to pay), because the Developer holds the full 2D drawing set, measures and
prices every project, and runs the whole chain (estimate → procurement → site → cost control) that
the single project dataset serves. The founding team's university network already reaches Developers
who are waiting to see the product, so the first clients are reachable without a cold market.

Consultants and contractors are later customers of the same dataset.
