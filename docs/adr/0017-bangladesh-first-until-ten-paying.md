# Bangladesh only until ten paying Developers, five of them repeat; markets as data from day one

After the founding clients, Vextrus grows in Bangladesh through three channels:
- references from the founding clients, from the team's network;
- REHAB (the Real Estate & Housing Association of Bangladesh), whose fairs and events are where the
  Sample Project is shown;
- design consultants, one of whom serves many Developers, and who become customers later
  (ADR 0001).

No second market opens until 10 Developers pay and at least 5 of them buy a second project. The next
market is then chosen by research. The Gulf is the current front-runner: its mid-market still runs
on 2D drawings, many of its engineers come from South Asia, business is in English, and willingness
to pay is higher. India comes later (crowded and price-sensitive). The UK and Australia come last:
they have BIM mandates and their own standards (NRM2, AIQS), so entering them is a different product.

From day one, everything that differs between countries is held as data, never hard-coded to
Bangladesh: Rule Sets, Rate Analyses, currency, Display Units and language. That costs almost nothing
now and would cost a rewrite later.
