# M2 — The whole building (the showcase)

Draft of 26 Sep 2026, for the owner's sign-off. Terms are `CONTEXT.md`'s. Decisions cited are ADRs
0002, 0003, 0005–0011, 0013–0016, 0022, 0026–0028, 0031 and 0033, `docs/data-model.md` with the
owner's §6 rulings, `docs/specs/bd-defaults.md`, and the Project Summary and BOQ grid rulings in
`docs/design/screens.md`. It assumes M0 (drawings in) and M1 (the frame and piles, priced) are done
as their own specs say. It references what they built and does not specify it again.

**What this spec does not know yet.** No Held-out Set has been obtained, and no Hand Takeoff exists
for the Edison set or any Held-out Set. Walls are the least-proven family we have: the session-10
prototype found about 60–75 % of walls (judged by eye), and on the Sample Project it found 0 of 20
ground-floor walls without a layer map (docs/research/2d-to-bim-prototype-lessons.md,
sample-project-first-read.md). Openings, stairs, tanks and foundations other than piles have never
been read by any of our prototypes. The AIT rate is not yet confirmed from NBR. The default
consumption ranges (cement, bricks, concrete per sft), the MEP lump-sum ranges and the starter
Developer's Specification have no primary source and need the owner's values. 3D on a phone has not
been measured.

## Problem Statement

After M1 the Developer's QS can take off and price the frame and the piles. The MD sees a
whole-building figure, but most of it is still allowance: excavation, masonry, plaster, floors,
doors and windows, stairs, tanks, roof, site works and services are all ৳/sft guesses. The MD cannot
take that figure to the board. It has not been compared with PWD. It carries no preliminaries,
contingency or tax. It cannot be issued and held still. It cannot be opened on a phone, or sent to a
partner as a model. It cannot be reworked in the Excel file a Dhaka office lives in.

The QS still measures the architectural half of the building by hand. That half has the worst
drafting: walls drawn as broken line pairs, door leaves and furniture that look like walls, the same
wall drawn twice. It also depends on the Developer's own finish choices, which the consultant's
drawings rarely state.

M2 is the milestone an MD is shown (docs/milestones.md). Two things must be true of it. The figure
must be whole and honest, and it must be proven on drawings the team never opened. And nothing on
screen, on paper or in Excel may look like a prototype. Vextrus Cubit lost its demos to test
artefacts, raw CAD codes and a sheet that opened looking empty (docs/postmortem.md, cause 5).

## Solution

At the end of M2 the QS runs all fourteen Takeoff Steps on a real Drawing Set. The machine proposes
and the QS confirms in bulk, answering Questions at the point of need (ADR 0007). The steps M2 adds
are:
- **Step 5 completed.** Mat foundations, footings, grade beams, basement walls, ramps and slab on
  grade. Earthwork (excavation, backfill, sand filling, soling, blinding) is derived by Measurement
  Rules, never read.
- **Step 9.** Stairs with landings and landing beams, and the lift pit.
- **Step 10.** Tanks.
- **Step 11.** Walls and openings, proposed as candidates that the machine filters for door leaves,
  furniture and double readings. The room-enclosure Check proves them. Their heights and deductions
  come from the confirmed structure. Every opening has a lintel, or a stated reason why it has none.
  Parapets and verandah fills are part of this step.
- **Step 12.** Rooms, with finishes, doors and windows from the Developer's Specification by room
  type.
- **Step 13.** The roof.
- **Step 14.** Site works and MEP as Lump Sums from a template. Each template line has a ৳/sft
  sanity range and must be entered or marked "not in this project".

By the end of the Takeoff every step's allowance has been replaced by its measured figure. The
Priced BOQ is measured throughout, except the Lump Sums and Provisional Sums the QS typed, which are
shown as such. On top of that:
- **The PWD Benchmark Rate** sits beside every working rate, as printed and net of PWD's mark-ups.
- **The Rule Set can be read and edited.** A rule edit never moves a figure silently. It is an
  explicit re-measure, previewed as a comparison and accepted by the QS.
- **The Estimate is layered.** Direct cost, then preliminaries and site overheads, contingency, and
  VAT and AIT on Labour Contract bills at dated rates.
- **Issuing freezes the Estimate** with its four pins: the facts version, the Drawing Set State, the
  Rule Set version and the Market Price set. The Market Price set is copied on issue.
- **Every comparison is split** into quantity effect and price effect against an Issued Estimate the
  MD picks.

The MD opens the Project Summary on a desktop or on a 390 px phone. It shows the Estimate, cost per
sft of Gross Floor Area and of Saleable Area, and the Target Cost warning tested on the full
Estimate. It also shows ৳ by Construction Stage, consumption checks per sft of Gross Floor Area,
"likely over allowance" flags while a Takeoff is still running, the latest comparison and the
Building Model in 3D. The MD can then:
- download the Excel file, whose five sheets carry live formulas that reproduce the screen to the
  paisa, or the PDF;
- send a read-only 3D share link that opens on a phone;
- ask the Level 2 assistant, in English or Bangla script, questions such as "concrete on the 3rd
  floor?" or "what if rebar rises 8 %?". Jev chooses what the question means, and code computes
  every number, with its Trace.

M2 is proven by the finish line below: all five Hand Takeoff zones on the Edison set and on two
Held-out Sets, scored blind, with Questions per step measured and a timed Takeoff by a QS who did not
build the product.

### The boundary: what is truly M2, and why
The test for every item: does an MD need it to believe the figure and act on it, or does the QS need
it to finish the whole building? What follows from that:
- **In: every Takeoff Step M1 left out.** Without them the figure stays mostly allowance, and the MD
  is shown a guess. Masonry, plaster and finishes are, by the Rate Analysis arithmetic, a large share
  of a Dhaka building's direct cost after the frame.
- **In: the Benchmark, the layers, issuing and the quantity/price split.** Without them an MD cannot
  check the figure against PWD, cannot see the true all-in cost, and cannot hold a figure still. Rebar
  moves 20–30 % in a year (ADR 0028).
- **In, but trimmed: the editable Rule Set.** Editing covers a rule's parameters (thresholds, working
  space, lintel bearing, lap multiplier, rounding places) and a choice among alternatives Vextrus
  ships (the joint rule F1 or F1-alt; 10" brickwork in cft or sft). No new rules and no formula
  language: that would be a second product, and every rule must stay one tested pure function
  (ADR 0031 §1). This is open question 4.
- **In, but trimmed: comparisons.** M2 builds the comparison engine and its baselines (Issued
  Estimates, the working state, a rule re-measure, a Market Price change). Element-by-element
  matching of a re-issued Drawing Set is M4 (ADR 0015), which plugs into the same engine.
- **In, but trimmed: the share link.** It shows the Building Model only: no ৳, no sheets, no Trace
  into the drawings. A link can leak, and the sheets are the consultant's content. This is open
  question 3.
- **In, but trimmed: the Level 2 assistant.** It answers from a fixed catalogue of eight query
  templates, compound price what-ifs included. Anything open-ended gets "not supported yet" and
  suggestions; open questions are Level 3 (ADR 0011).
- **In, as typed Lump Sums only: site works and MEP.** A measured boundary wall or drain waits.
  ADR 0003 keeps MEP as lump sums, and ADR 0007 puts site works in the same template.
- **Out: rebar from the drawing for any new family.** Mats, stairs, tanks, lintels and walls carry
  rebar by ratio in M2. Beams and slabs are M3 (ADR 0010).
- **Out: PWD's added rates per floor and per metre of height.** The data model leaves them out of
  the MVP (docs/data-model.md §7). The Benchmark shows PWD's base rate and says so.
- **Out: consumption ranges derived from past projects.** No Developer has past projects in
  Vextrus yet. The Developer may type its own ranges.

### What the demo must never show
The showcase is the Sample Project (ADR 0004: "the sample clients see first"), taken off through the
running product by a person, never seeded from fixtures. The Edison set is shown only with the
owner's permission, and a Held-out Set never. On any screen, export or share link of the showcase,
none of these may appear:
1. **Test artefacts:** "test", "foo", "lorem", fixture or seed names, example.com users, TODO, draft
   watermarks other than "Working — not issued", or a debug panel.
2. **Raw CAD codes:** `%%C`, `%%D` and `%%P` undecoded; layer names ("A-WALL-5in") or block names
   ("*U123", "$0$") used as labels; entity handles; drawing-unit numbers shown as if they were
   feet.
3. **Internal vocabulary:** item codes ("RCC-COL-1:1.5:3") where the item number (2.1.1) and its
   description belong; "facts_seq", "RSv3", "rod_ratio", UUIDs, enum spellings; the word "rod".
4. **An empty-looking sheet:**
   - a drawing that opens as a speck or a blank;
   - a grid, tile or Excel sheet with no rows and no reason given;
   - a 3D view with no model;
   - a Summary tile showing ৳0 where the figure is unknown. Unknown shows "—" with a reason
     (docs/design/system.md §6–7).
5. **Number defects:**
   - NaN, `undefined`, `null`, 0.000 in place of "—";
   - unrounded floats;
   - lakh grouping on a coordinate or dimension;
   - a level spelled two ways;
   - "1 columns";
   - a ৳ total that does not add up to its parts.
6. **Anything unfinished:**
   - an open Question or a non-empty "Unclassified" heading on the showcase project;
   - a stack trace, a console error or a spinner over a table;
   - a desktop-only screen squeezed onto a phone without its plain "desktop only" notice.

A `cloud` hygiene test scans the showcase's API responses and exports for these patterns. The
`product-review` walk checks them by eye at 1440, 1280 and 390 px.

## User Stories

### Foundations, substructure and earthwork
1. As a QS, I want the mats, footings, grade beams, basement walls, ramps and slab on grade proposed from the foundation plans and their schedules, so that I confirm the substructure the way I confirmed the frame.
2. As a QS, I want each footing and mat schedule checked against its plan (n / N, N from the drawing), so that a size the two disagree on becomes one Question, not a silent wrong figure.
3. As a QS, I want a mat's thickenings, drops and depressions (the lift pit, deepenings under columns) proposed or asked, so that a mat is never measured as a flat slab when it is not.
4. As a QS, I want excavation, backfill, sand filling in plinth, brick flat soling and blinding derived by Measurement Rules from the confirmed foundations, so that I never measure earthwork by hand, and it follows every change to a foundation.
5. As a QS, I want to be asked once for the existing ground level and the plinth level when the drawings do not state them, so that every earthwork figure traces to a drawing or to my answer.
6. As a QS, I want each earthwork Measurement Line to name its rule and its parameter (450 mm working space, 3" blinding projection, 3" thickness), so that I can defend the figure to a contractor.
7. As a QS, I want excavation split into 1.5 m depth stages, so that the lines match PWD's items and the Benchmark sits beside the right one.
8. As a QS, I want brickwork below plinth billed as its own item, apart from superstructure brickwork, so that the Priced BOQ follows PWD's split.
9. As a QS, I want the backfill Check to flag a negative or implausible backfill, so that a wrong ground level or a missing foundation shows at once.
10. As a QS, I want dewatering and shoring offered as template lines I price or mark "not needed", so that a site with a high water table is not silently under-priced.

### Stairs, lift pit and tanks
11. As a QS, I want each stair flight proposed between two confirmed storeys, with its riser, tread, waist and width from the stair section and plan, so that I confirm a whole stair core in a few keystrokes.
12. As a QS, I want landings measured as slab and landing beams as beams, so that the stair follows the Rule Set (S1) and PWD's classes.
13. As a QS, I want risers × riser height checked against the storey height from the confirmed levels, so that a misread riser count becomes a Question.
14. As a QS, I want the lift pit (its slab and walls below the lowest floor) proposed from the section, with a Question when its depth is not drawn, so that the pit is never forgotten.
15. As a QS, I want the underground water reservoir and the overhead tank proposed with their walls, base and cover slab, so that the tanks are measured like any RCC element.
16. As a QS, I want a tank's internal volume checked against a capacity stated in the notes, so that a misread wall height or plan size is caught.
17. As a QS, I want a septic tank read when the structural set draws it, and otherwise a template line, so that it is measured when it can be and never forgotten when it cannot.
18. As a QS, I want the rebar of stairs, tanks, mats and the lift pit shown "by ratio" with its Rebar Ratio, so that I and the MD know it is not yet from the drawing.

### Walls and openings
19. As a QS, I want wall runs proposed for every storey from the architectural plans, registered on the confirmed grid and running clear between confirmed columns, so that masonry fills the frame I already confirmed.
20. As a QS, I want door leaves, swing arcs, furniture, sanitary fixtures and hatch boundaries filtered out and shown as "filtered" with the reason, so that I can restore one the machine wrongly dropped.
21. As a QS, I want a wall read twice (from two views, from a block and its exploded copy, or from an enlarged plan) kept once, with the double reading named, so that no wall is billed twice.
22. As a QS, I want each wall's thickness class (5", 10") proposed from its minimum-rectangle width, and an odd thickness asked, so that no wall lands in the wrong BOQ Item.
23. As a QS, I want a wall's height to come from the confirmed structure (the soffit of the beam or slab above), so that I never type a wall height and it follows any change to a beam depth.
24. As a QS, I want a wall's Trace to name the beam or slab its height came from, so that a disputed wall height is settled on the sheet.
25. As a QS, I want the room-enclosure Check to test my wall Proposals against every room label, and to put a revision cloud where a room does not close, so that a missing or phantom wall becomes a Question before I confirm.
26. As a QS, I want the walls of a typical floor plan applied to its Storey Band, with a storey's exceptions raised as Questions, so that I confirm ten floors once and the odd floor on its own.
27. As a QS, I want wall Proposals grouped by Storey Band and thickness class, with "confirm all agreeing", so that a step of several thousand wall runs takes minutes, not hours.
28. As a QS, I want openings proposed from their tags and door and window blocks, with sizes from the door and window schedule, so that every opening has a size with a Trace.
29. As a QS, I want the door and window schedule checked against the tags on the plans, as n / N per type, so that a missing or extra opening becomes a Question.
30. As a QS, I want each opening hosted in exactly one confirmed wall, so that its deduction lands in the right wall and storey.
31. As a QS, I want openings over 0.1 m² deducted from brickwork as their own visible deduction line, so that my measurement sheet reads like one I would write.
32. As a QS, I want a lintel proposed over each opening by rule L1 (the opening width plus 6" bearing each side), with a stated reason required for every opening without one (a beam soffit at the head, an RCC wall, a frame-only opening, or a reason I type), so that no lintel is silently missed or added.
33. As a QS, I want the concrete embedded in a wall (lintels, beam ends, columns) deducted by the junction-ownership rule, so that masonry and concrete never overlap in the figures or in 3D.
34. As a QS, I want parapets on the roof and verandahs, and verandah fills, proposed with their heights from the sections or asked, so that the low walls are measured like the full ones.
35. As a QS, I want 10" brickwork billed in cft and 5" in sft, with bricks in nos in the Material Schedule, so that the Priced BOQ uses Dhaka's units (ADR 0008).
36. As a QS, I want a sparse ground floor or parking level (few walls, a guard room, a driveway) proposed as reliably as a typical floor, so that the podium zone is not the weak zone.

### Rooms, finishes, doors and windows
37. As a QS, I want rooms proposed as the regions my confirmed walls enclose, each bound to its label text, so that rooms come free once the walls are right.
38. As a QS, I want each room's type (bed, toilet, kitchen, living, dining, verandah, lobby, stair, lift, parking, utility, guard) proposed with a confidence, and shown as a choice when unsure, so that a label like "M. BED" or "ATT. TOILET" still lands right.
39. As a QS, I want a room's measured area checked against the dimension text written in it (for example 12'-0" × 14'-0"), so that a misplaced wall is caught by the architect's own figure.
40. As a QS, I want the Developer's Specification applied to every confirmed room by its type (floor, skirting, wall, ceiling, doors, windows, fittings), so that finishes are measured without my typing a finish per room.
41. As a QS, I want wall finishes in height bands (toilet tiles to 7'-0", plaster and paint above), so that one room's wall carries the right mix of items.
42. As a QS, I want plaster deductions by the three IS 1200 bands (up to 0.5 m² none; up to 3 m² one face; over 3 m² both faces plus jambs, soffits and sills), so that plaster matches the Rule Set I signed.
43. As a QS, I want outer wall faces measured under the "exterior" entry of the Specification (outer plaster 1:4 and exterior paint), so that the façade is not forgotten because no room owns it.
44. As a QS, I want a room type with no Specification line raised as a Question, so that no room is silently left unfinished.
45. As a QS, I want one room's Specification overridden (for example the master bath's premium tiles) as a recorded, traced exception, so that I don't fork the Developer's Specification for one room.
46. As a QS, I want doors and windows priced per sft from the Specification by the room they serve (main entrance, bedroom, toilet, kitchen, verandah grill), so that the Doors & Windows section fills from confirmed openings.
47. As a QS, I want stair treads and risers, landings and railings finished from the "stair" entry of the Specification, so that the stair core is finished like any room.
48. As a QS, I want floor finish measured between wall faces before skirting and plaster, skirting in rft, dado in sft and painting on the plaster it covers, so that the rules match IS 1200 as the Rule Set states them.

### Roof
49. As a QS, I want roof treatment measured over the confirmed roof slabs inside the parapets, with its upturn, so that waterproofing is billed from the model.
50. As a QS, I want the stair room and the lift machine room at the roof taken off with the same wall, room and finish steps, so that the roof zone is whole.
51. As a QS, I want rainwater outlets counted and downpipes measured by rule over the building height, so that the small roof items are not a guess.

### Site works and MEP
52. As a QS, I want the MEP and site works template (plumbing and sanitary, electrical, lifts, generator, substation, fire, pumps, intercom and CCTV, gas; boundary wall and gate, drains, paving, guard house, soil test) as lines I type an amount into, so that nothing expected is forgotten.
53. As a QS, I want each Lump Sum's ৳ per sft of Gross Floor Area shown against its sanity range and flagged outside it, so that a slip of a zero is caught.
54. As a QS, I want to mark a template line "not in this project" with a reason, and see any line left neither entered nor excluded, so that completeness is visible.
55. As a QS, I want to add a Provisional Sum at the end of the BOQ Section it provides for, kept outside the measured subtotal, so that unknown scope is honest.
56. As a QS, I want a changed Lump Sum treated as a price effect unless I mark it a scope change, so that comparisons tell the MD why it moved (BOQ grid ruling 4).

### Coverage, Checks and the Takeoff as a whole
57. As a QS, I want Coverage to account for every view on every sheet (used by a step, or excluded with a reason), so that at the end of M2 nothing in the Drawing Set is silently unread.
58. As a QS, I want every M2 Check listed per step with its n / N, so that I can see what has been proven and what has not.
59. As a QS, I want Checks to re-run at every Confirmation (a changed beam depth moves wall heights and plaster), so that a late edit cannot leave a stale figure.
60. As a QS, I want the Questions tab sorted by their effect on quantity or ৳, so that I answer the expensive ones first (ADR 0011 rule 6).
61. As a QS, I want every step's allowance replaced whole by its measured figure when the step is confirmed, so that the measured share rises step by step to 100 % of the measured work.
62. As a QS, I want the measured Gross Floor Area (to the outside of the confirmed walls) to replace the area I entered, marked as the change, so that cost per sft and allowances rest on the model.
63. As a QS, I want the new families in 3D as I confirm them (walls with their openings cut, stairs, tanks, parapets), coloured by status, so that I see the building complete.

### The Rule Set
64. As a QS, I want to read the Developer's Rule Set in words, grouped by family, with each rule's source (IS 1200, PWD, practice) and its parameters, so that I can check it as I would a method of measurement.
65. As a QS, I want to edit a rule's parameter or choose one of its shipped alternatives, saved as a draft, then published as a new version, so that the Rule Set matches my Developer's habits.
66. As a QS, I want a project to keep its pinned Rule Set version until I accept a re-measure, so that no published edit ever moves a figure silently.
67. As a QS, I want the re-measure previewed as a comparison showing only the quantity effect on the items the edit touches, so that I know what I am accepting.
68. As a QS, I want Rebar Ratios and diameter splits edited the same way, so that one mechanism governs every measuring choice.
69. As a QS, I want every Measurement Line's Trace to name the rules and the Rule Set version that produced it, so that a disputed figure points to a rule.
70. As a QS, I want an edit that changes a Billing Unit compared by converting the baseline quantity exactly, so that a unit switch never looks like a quantity change.

### The Benchmark Rate
71. As a QS, I want the PWD Benchmark Rate beside every working rate, as printed and net of PWD's mark-ups (10 % profit, 3.5 % overhead, 10 % VAT: printed ≈ 1.2611 × net), with the PWD code, edition and zone, so that I compare like with like.
72. As a QS, I want the Benchmark converted exactly into the item's Billing Unit, so that a per-cum rate sits correctly beside a per-cft rate.
73. As a QS, I want an item with no PWD equivalent to show "—" with the reason, so that a missing benchmark is not read as zero.
74. As a QS, I want an item covered by a Labour Contract benchmarked on its share of that contract, allocated in proportion to the labour its own Rate Analysis would have carried, so that covered items are not shown falsely cheap.
75. As a QS, I want a note that the Benchmark is PWD's base rate, without its per-floor added rates, so that nobody reads an upper-floor comparison as exact.

### The Estimate's layers
76. As a QS, I want preliminaries and site overheads entered as items or as a % of direct cost, so that the Estimate carries what the site costs to run.
77. As a QS, I want a contingency % on the Estimate, so that the MD sees the reserve as its own line.
78. As a QS, I want VAT and AIT applied to Labour Contract bills at rates held as dated data, so that tax follows the law in force on the Estimate's date and not a number I typed.
79. As a QS, I want each layer shown with its base and rate, and the total as direct cost plus layers, so that the build-up is auditable.
80. As an MD, I want the cost per sft quoted on the full Estimate with the direct cost per sft beside it, so that I compare with what I know from past buildings.

### Issued Estimates
81. As a QS or an MD, I want to issue the Estimate with an issue number and date, so that I have a figure I can take to the board.
82. As an MD, I want an Issued Estimate never to change after later edits to prices, rules, Rate Analyses or the Takeoff, so that the board's figure holds.
83. As a QS, I want the Market Price set frozen on issue and a new working set copied from it, so that I keep editing prices without touching the issued figure.
84. As a QS, I want the list of Issued Estimates with their pins in words ("Rule Set version 4; Market Prices of 12 Oct 2026; Takeoff as of step 12 confirmed"), so that I can say what each issue rests on.
85. As an MD, I want an Estimate issued mid-Takeoff to print its measured share and open Questions, so that an early issue is never mistaken for a final one.
86. As an MD, I want to issue from the Project Summary, so that I don't need the QS's screens (Summary ruling 8).

### Comparisons
87. As an MD, I want to pick any Issued Estimate as the baseline and see the working Estimate against it, so that I see what changed since I last reported.
88. As an MD, I want each change split into quantity effect and price effect that add up exactly to the change in ৳, so that I know whether the building or the market moved.
89. As an MD, I want the changed elements listed (new, removed, changed) with their quantity effect, so that I can ask the QS about a specific wall or column.
90. As an MD, I want steps still on allowance listed as "still on allowance", and steps measured since the baseline shown as "measured replaces allowance", so that a basis change is never mistaken for a design change (Summary ruling 2).
91. As a QS, I want a Market Price edit previewed against the latest Issued Estimate as a price effect, so that I can warn the MD before a price change lands.

### The Project Summary
92. As an MD, I want tiles on the desktop for the Estimate with its measured-share bar, cost per sft of Gross Floor Area, cost per sft of Saleable Area and the Target Cost, so that I read the project in one glance.
93. As an MD, I want a fixed brief as the first screen on my phone at 390 px, so that I can read it between meetings.
94. As an MD, I want to set a Target Cost and be warned, with a triangle and "Over Target Cost by ৳…", when the full Estimate (layers included, allowances included) crosses it, even while the Takeoff is running, so that I hear bad news early.
95. As an MD, I want cost by trade in work order and by floor (labelled as shares), with allowance hatched, so that I see where the money is.
96. As an MD, I want ৳ by Construction Stage with the Estimate's layers spread pro rata, so that I see what each slab casting and stage will cost.
97. As an MD, I want a building-section panel showing each floor with its slab casting's Construction Stage, so that the stages read as the building I know.
98. As an MD, I want consumption checks per sft of Gross Floor Area (rebar kg, cement bags, bricks, concrete cft) against ranges, flagged amber when outside and never blocked, so that I can sanity-check the QS's work the way I always have.
99. As an MD, I want an amber "likely over allowance" flag when a step's measured-so-far, projected to its n / N, passes its allowance, so that I see the overrun before the step is done, without the Estimate changing.

101. As an MD, I want what is confirmed and what is open (steps, Questions, the share of rebar from the drawing) on the Summary, so that I know how far the Takeoff has got.

### Excel and PDF

## Implementation Decisions

Modules and interfaces follow docs/architecture.md: higher layers import lower, siblings stay
independent, and only `takeoff`'s confirm service writes `building_model`. A cross-module feature
is two slices, the lower module's service first.

### `engine`: the new Element Families, as packages
- **New family packages,** each with recognise, check, geometry and measure, plus its family row
  as data (ADR 0031 §5). A family ticket only adds its own package and row.
  - `mat`, `footing`, `grade_beam`, `basement_wall`, `ramp` and `slab_on_grade` (step 5, if M1
    did not take them).
  - `stair` and `lift_pit` (step 9).
  - `tank` (step 10).
  - `wall` and `opening` (step 11).
  - `room` (step 12).
  - `roof_finish` (step 13).
- **Earthwork is measured, not recognised.** It is a measure-only package over the confirmed
  foundations and two site facts, the existing ground level and the plinth level. Those facts are
  confirmed Elements of the `spec_note` family: from the drawing where stated, otherwise the answer
  to a Question.
- **Identity rules,** per family (ADR 0015):
  - stair: stair core + storey pair;
  - lift pit: its core;
  - tank: kind + position;
  - wall: axis overlap within its storey;
  - opening: host wall + position along it;
  - room: polygon overlap within its storey;
  - foundations: position + outline overlap.
  A mark or a room label is a hint, never the key.
- **A lintel is a fact of its opening, not an Element.** Its facts are either a section and a
  bearing, from rule L1, or a reason for having no lintel. It shares the opening's identity, and
  its concrete, formwork and rebar still go to their own BOQ Items (class c). Why: a lintel has no
  identity apart from its opening, and a separate family would double the Confirmations.
- **Parapets and verandah fills are wall kinds, not families.** Their Billing Units and heights
  follow the wall rules, with their own height facts.
- **Wall recognition** follows the evidence (sample-project-first-read.md):
  - candidates are parallel line pairs and outlines on the layers the per-sheet layer map confirms;
  - thickness is the minimum-rectangle width, never 2·area/perimeter;
  - nested blocks are exploded;
  - DIMLFAC and insert scale are honoured.
- **The wall filters,** each recorded on the candidate with its reason so the QS can restore it:
  - door leaves: a line pair inside a door block, or tangent to a swing arc whose radius matches
    the opening width;
  - furniture and fixtures: a layer or block the map classes as such, or a block kind Jev picks;
  - hatch boundaries;
  - double readings: candidates with the same identity under the wall rule, from two views or from
    a block and its exploded copy.
- **Heights and deductions come from the confirmed structure.**
  - A wall's top is the soffit of the confirmed beam along its axis (within a tolerance
    parameter), or else the soffit of the slab above.
  - Masonry deducts embedded concrete through the same junction-ownership function that shapes the
    3D (ADR 0009). The conservation Check extends to masonry: wall and concrete volumes owned in a
    storey sum exactly to their union.
- **Room regions** are the faces of the planar arrangement of confirmed wall faces, column faces,
  RCC walls and openings, closed across openings. Each region is bound to the label text inside it,
  by code. Jev only picks the room type from the label text, over the Developer's Specification's
  room types plus "not a room".
- **Measuring stays pure:** `measure(confirmed facts, Rule Set version) → Measurement Lines`.
  - Each Measurement Line carries Nos, L, B, H (or an area), its quantity rounded to 2 dp in the
    Billing Unit, its rule codes, storey and stage kind.
  - **Deductions are their own negative Measurement Lines** ("Deduct: opening D2"), as a QS writes
    them. This makes a rule visible and lets Excel show Nos × L × B × H honestly.
- **The M2 Measurement Rules.** Each family package carries its rules and BOQ Items as data, each
  rule with a source label:
  - E1–E4 (earthwork, soling, blinding);
  - P3 (grade beams);
  - S1 (stairs);
  - W1–W6 (walls);
  - L1 (lintels);
  - PL1–PL5 (plaster);
  - FL1–FL2 (floors and skirting);
  - PT1 (painting);
  - new rules for tanks, the lift pit, roof treatment, rainwater goods, DPC, doors and windows
    (area = the schedule's width × height; frames in rft), railings and stage assignment.

  The Library's default Rule Set version for M2 is assembled from the packages and published once
  the owner signs it. The Hand Takeoffs are measured under that signed version (ADR 0005).
- **3D geometry for the new families** is parametric, like the frame's (ADR 0022):
  - walls are extruded between their base and their structural top, with openings cut;
  - stairs have flights and landings;
  - tanks are open boxes with covers.

  The browser builds the geometry, and a CI test checks it against the server's per-element volume
  and bounds. Rooms get no solid; they are shown as a floor tint when selected.

### `takeoff`
- **Steps 5 (the rest) and 9–14** are wired into the M1 Takeoff screen and step rail. Nothing new
  in the screen's grammar; there are new inspector panels:
  - **walls:** grouped by Storey Band and thickness class, with a "filtered" layer toggle on the
    sheet;
  - **openings:** grouped by tag, with the schedule's n / N;
  - **the lintel-reason card:** radio rows of the reasons code found, then "other: type a reason";
  - **rooms:** grouped by type, with Jev's pick and chips when unsure, and a preview of the
    Specification's ৳ for the room;
  - **roof:** its treatment picked from the Specification's "roof" entry;
  - **step 14:** the template form with ৳/sft against range.
- **The room-enclosure Check runs inside step 11 on wall Proposals,** seeded by the room labels.
  It is how walls are proven: a room that does not close raises a Question at the gap ("Room BED 2,
  Level 4, is open between grid B and C: a missing wall, an opening, or not a room?"). Step 12
  then confirms rooms and their types.
- **The Developer's Specification.**
  - It is owned by the Developer and pinned per project through the takeoff setup.
  - It is widened from one item per (room type, surface) to an ordered list per surface, each entry
    with an optional height band. Toilet walls, for example, are tiles to 7'-0", then plaster 1:6
    and paint above.
  - There is an "exterior" entry for outer faces, and "stair" and "roof" entries.
  - One room may override its Specification, recorded and traced as `developer_specification`
    with the override.
  - Vextrus ships a starter Specification for a typical Dhaka mid-range apartment. Its values are
    for the owner to verify (Further Notes).
- **Applying the Specification is a measure, not a Confirmation.** Confirmed rooms, walls and
  openings, with the pinned Specification, give the finish Measurement Lines on read. Editing the
  Specification therefore re-prices without re-confirming. It still shows as a quantity effect in
  any comparison, because the finishes' items change.
- **The M2 Check catalogue entries,** each with a code, version, family and words, and milestone M2.
  - **Against the source:**
    - footing and mat schedule against plan;
    - riser count × riser height against storey height;
    - lift-pit depth against the section;
    - tank volume against the stated capacity;
    - door and window schedule against the plan tags (n / N per type);
    - each opening hosted in one wall;
    - room enclosure;
    - room area against the room's dimension text;
    - the measured Gross Floor Area against the drawing's area statement, where one exists;
    - filtered candidates audited (every door-leaf filter sits at a confirmed opening; otherwise a
      Question).
  - **Conservation:**
    - masonry and concrete volumes sum to their union;
    - no two confirmed walls overlap beyond a tolerance (the double-reading guard);
    - backfill equals excavation minus the buried volume, and is never negative;
    - each item's labour comes from exactly one source, extended to the M2 items.
  - **Completeness:**
    - every opening has a lintel or a reason;
    - every room type has a Specification entry for every surface;
    - every Lump Sum template line is entered or excluded with a reason;
    - Coverage has zero unaccounted views.
  - **Sanity ranges (flags, never blocks):**
    - consumption per sft of Gross Floor Area;
    - Lump Sum ৳/sft;
    - wall thickness classes.
- **The new Jev nodes,** each shipped with a spot check of about 30 real items, a measured queue
  size and a pinned model version (ADR 0011 rule 3): room type from label text; block kind (door,
  window, fixture, furniture) for unknown block names; the residue of opening-tag binding, given
  ranks and not raw millimetres. Every other decision in M2 is code.

### `measurement`
- **Rule Set editing:**
  - `draft(rule_set) → version`;
  - `set_param(version, rule_code, name, value)`, with the value checked against the parameter's
    typed range held beside the rule;
  - `choose_alternative(version, rule_code, alternative)`;
  - `publish(version)`, which makes it immutable with a content hash, then offers it to every
    project pinned to an older version.
  - The words of a rule are rendered from its template and parameters, so they can never disagree
    with what is measured.
- **Re-measure:**
  - `remeasure_preview(project, to_version)` returns a `revisions` comparison of kind
    `rule_remeasure`: a quantity effect only.
  - `accept_remeasure(project, to_version)` closes the old pin and opens the new one, and records
    who accepted it.
  - A Billing Unit change converts the baseline quantity exactly into the new unit.
- **Rebar Ratios and diameter splits** are edited through the same draft, publish and pin path.
  The project fork used for Storey Band overrides (M1) is unchanged.

### `rates`
- **The Benchmark.**
  - `benchmark(item_code, zone, edition, billing_unit) → {printed, net, pwd_code, edition, zone,
    page}`, where net = printed ÷ the edition's mark-up factor (1.135 ÷ (1 − VAT) = 1.2611 for the
    SoR 2022 2nd Revised edition, in force from 22 Jan 2026).
  - The rate is converted exactly into the Billing Unit.
  - Items without a PWD equivalent return "none" with a reason.
  - A covered item's comparison figure is its allocated share of the Labour Contract (the §6
    ruling 3).
  - The Library holds BenchmarkRate and BenchmarkMarkup rows for every default BOQ Item. Each row
    cites its SoR page.
- **Tax rates** are dated TaxRate rows: VAT 10 % from the edition's date, with 7.5 % before it; AIT
  is pending NBR. `tax_rate(kind, applies_to, on_date)`.
- **Freezing prices.** `freeze_set(set_id)` is called by issuing, and the next edit copies the set
  (ADR 0028 as ruled). Every price keeps its last-changed date.
- **The library extension:** Resources and Rate Analyses for every M2 BOQ Item. They are derived
  the same way as the first 18, from PWD's published relations, each with its confidence, and the
  owner verifies them.
  - Doors, windows, grills, paint and waterproofing ship as Material-and-Labour Contract items
    (supply and fix, one rate per unit). They stay out of the Material Schedule, which matches the
    owner's verified list of materials.
  - Tiles stay in the Material Schedule.
  - All of this is practice, at Low confidence, for the owner to verify.

### `boq`
- **Estimate Layers:**
  - an ordered, editable list per project, with Library defaults;
  - preliminaries and site overheads as items or a % of direct cost; contingency as a % of the
    subtotal; VAT and AIT as a % of the Labour Contract amounts (the base is open question 2);
  - each layer is computed on the rounded amounts (the §6 ruling 4);
  - `estimate(project, as_of) → {direct by BOQ Section and by Trade, measured subtotal, Lump Sums,
    Provisional Sums, each layer with its base, grand total, per sft of Gross Floor Area and of
    Saleable Area}`.
- **Issuing:**
  - `issue(project, by) → IssuedEstimate` writes the frozen rows (IssuedLine, IssuedLineStorey,
    IssuedRateLine, IssuedMaterial, IssuedLayer) with the four pins;
  - it records the measured share and the number of open Questions;
  - its issue number runs in sequence per project.
  - `issued(project, issue_no)` never recomputes: it reads the frozen rows. A test asserts that
    they equal a recomputation at the pins.
- **Lump Sums and Provisional Sums from the template:**
  - LumpSumTemplateLine has a sanity range in ৳/sft of Gross Floor Area and a BOQ Section
    (Services or External works);
  - LumpSum gains a `scope_change` mark, for comparisons, and an `excluded_reason` for "not in this
    project".
- **৳ by Construction Stage.** Stage kinds for the new families are Rule Set `stage` rules:
  - stairs go to the slab casting of the storey above;
  - the underground reservoir and the lift pit go to substructure;
  - the overhead tank goes to the roof's casting;
  - walls, lintels, parapets and fills go to masonry;
  - finishes, doors and windows go to finishes;
  - MEP goes to services;
  - site works go to external works.

  Layers spread over the stages pro rata (Summary ruling 6).
- **"Likely over allowance"** is computed on read: for each step still on allowance, measured so far
  × N / n compared with the allowance. It is a flag only, and never changes the Estimate
  (Summary ruling 3).
- **The measured Gross Floor Area** comes from the confirmed Building Model (every storey's outline
  to the outside of the walls, basement and parking included) once step 11 is confirmed. Until then
  it is the area the QS entered, marked "entered".

### `revisions`
- **`compare(baseline, target) → Comparison`**, where baseline and target are each an Issued
  Estimate or a model state (facts version + Rule Set version + Market Price set). There is no
  Revision matching in M2.
- **The invariants,** per item:
  - quantity effect = (q1 − q0) × r0;
  - price effect = (r1 − r0) × q1;
  - their sum equals q1·r1 − q0·r0 exactly;
  - a new item has only a quantity effect, at r1; a removed item has only a quantity effect, of
    −q0·r0;
  - the Σ over items, layers and Lump Sums equals the change in the Estimate.
- **A step measured between baseline and target** is one row kind, "measured replaces allowance".
  The allowance's ৳ is removed and the measured items are added, both as quantity effect, so that
  the invariants still hold. Steps still on allowance at the target are listed "still on
  allowance", with no inside-allowance column (Summary ruling 2).
- **Lump Sums.** A changed amount is a price effect unless it is marked a scope change, when it is a
  quantity effect.
- **Element differences** (new, removed, changed facts) come from the Element States between the
  two facts versions, with the quantity effect of each.
- **Kinds:** `issued_vs_working`, `issued_vs_issued`, `rule_remeasure` and `price_update`. M4 adds
  `revision` to the same engine.

### `summary`
- **`project_summary(project, as_of: working | issue_no)`** is computed on read. It holds:
  - the tiles;
  - the phone brief;
  - the building-section panel;
  - cost by trade and by floor (as shares);
  - ৳ by stage;
  - consumption per sft of Gross Floor Area against its ConsumptionRange;
  - the Target Cost state, tested on the full Estimate;
  - likely-over-allowance flags;
  - floor ৳/sft, shown as "—" below a minimum floor area (a parameter; the plan sets its default);
  - the latest comparison;
  - Takeoff counts n / N;
  - the share of rebar from the drawing.
- **ConsumptionRange** rows can be edited per Developer, with Library defaults. The rebar default of
  4.5–6 kg/sft is ruled (bd-defaults). The cement, brick and concrete defaults are the owner's to
  set.
- **The MD** sets the Target Cost (`projects`), issues, and reads. The MD edits no Takeoff and no
  layer.
- **Layout:** A's tiles on the desktop, B's fixed brief as the phone's first screen, C's section as
  a panel (Summary ruling 1). It works from 390 px (ADR 0016).

### `exports`
- **Excel** (XlsxWriter, built on the server): `excel(project, issue_no | working) → file`, five
  sheets.
  1. **Summary:** direct cost by BOQ Section and by Trade as SUMIFs over the Abstract, then each
     layer as a formula on its base, the grand total, and cost per sft as formulas over area cells.
  2. **Abstract:**
     - the item number, description, Billing Unit, quantity (a sum over its Measurement rows) and
       rate (a reference to its Rate Analysis cell);
     - the amount as `=ROUND(qty*rate,2)`;
     - the Benchmark printed and net as values, with edition and zone;
     - the Cost Basis and Rebar Basis;
     - Lump Sums as quantity 1 at the typed amount;
     - Provisional Sums at the end of their BOQ Section;
     - any remaining allowance as consumption × Gross Floor Area × Market Price, in formulas.
  3. **Measurement:**
     - one row per Measurement Line: `=ROUND(Nos*L*B*H,2)` in the Billing Unit;
     - dimension cells carry the exact values the engine used, displayed in feet and inches;
     - deductions are negative rows;
     - each row names its rules and sheet and links to its Trace in the product.
  4. **Rate Analysis:**
     - a Market Prices block, one cell per Resource;
     - each item's lines as qty × (1 + wastage) × price;
     - the working rate as `=ROUND(SUM(...),2)`;
     - covered labour lines shown as "covered by" with 0.
  5. **Material Schedule:**
     - by floor and Construction Stage;
     - each cell is `=ROUNDUP(...)` of SUMIFS over the Measurement sheet × the coefficients;
     - the total is the sum of the rounded cells (what is bought, stage by stage).

  **Formats:**
  - lakh grouping through custom number formats taken from the shared table of expected formats;
  - negative money (comparisons) uses a conditional format, because an Excel custom format holds
    only two conditions;
  - every sheet's header carries the issue number and date, or "Working — not issued".
- **PDF** (WeasyPrint, the Vextrus Taka glyph embedded): the summary, the abstract and the Material
  Schedule, with the measurement sheet optional. It uses the same figures service as the screen.
- **An Export** records its input hash, the issue number or working, and who created it.
- **The share link:**
  - `create_link(project, pinned: issue_no | live, expires_at) → token` (only the hash is stored);
  - `revoke_link`;
  - `resolve(token)` goes through the single SECURITY DEFINER lookup, then runs read-only under
    that tenant (docs/data-model.md §3.6);
  - the payload is the GLB per storey built on the server, plus a per-element info table (family,
    mark, size in its kind, storey). No ৳, no Trace anchors, no sheets.
  - The phone viewer is the same three.js viewer in a touch layout (storey isolate, section slider,
    tap for info).

### `assistant`
- **`ask(project, text, user) → AskResult`**, with the route, the slots, a confidence, the chips,
  the answer and its figures (each with its Trace). The answer is rendered by a code template, in
  English.
- **The fixed catalogue:**
  1. a quantity by material, item, storey, stage or family;
  2. materials up to a Construction Stage;
  3. cost by trade, BOQ Section, floor or stage;
  4. cost per sft;
  5. a Market Price what-if (one Noul per Resource for compound asks), shown as a price-effect
     comparison and not saved;
  6. why this figure (Trace + rules);
  7. what changed since issue n;
  8. what is open.
- **Jev picks the route and the slots** in one request. Numbers come from regex candidates that Jev
  picks among, never types (docs/research/jev-system-one.md §4).
- **Every answer is shown as chips.** Below the auto-accept threshold, the answer waits for the
  chips to be confirmed. Banglish always waits for its chips. If Jev is unavailable, the chips start
  empty and the user fills them.
- **AskLog** records the text, route, Jev answer, service and user.
- **Where it lives:** the Ctrl K box on the desktop screens, and a box on the Project Summary on
  the phone too.

### `building_model` and `projects`
- **Family rows** for the new families, and their params in SI:
  - wall: axis, thickness class, base and top storey references, kind;
  - opening: host, position, width, height, sill, tag, lintel facts;
  - room: polygon, label, type;
  - stair: flights, risers, treads, waist, width;
  - tank: kind, outline, wall and base thickness, cover.
- **The site facts** (existing ground level, plinth level) are `spec_note` Elements.
- **`projects`** gains nothing new beyond M1's Target Cost, Saleable Area and entered area. The
  measured Gross Floor Area is computed by `boq` from the Building Model.

### Units, rounding and the grid
Units and rounding are unchanged from ADR 0008 and the §6 rulings: Measurement Lines to 2 dp, rebar
lines to 2 dp of a kg with the item in whole kg, amount = ROUND(qty × rate, 2), layers on the
rounded amounts, the Material Schedule rounded up. The BOQ grid (M1) shows the new columns (the
Benchmark, printed and net) and the Estimate's layers under the totals. A rate is still never typed
over its Rate Analysis.

## Testing Decisions

**What makes a good test here:**
- It tests behaviour at the highest seam that shows it, never internals.
- It uses real drawings for reading and synthetic fixtures only for mechanics, never offered as
  proof (docs/sdlc.md).

**The seams, highest first:**
1. **The HTTP API of each module,** on a Building Model built through the confirm service, never
   by writing to tables. This is the seam for money:
   - Estimate, layers, issue, comparisons, Summary, exports, share link and assistant are all
     tested here;
   - the figures service the screen uses is the one every export and the assistant are checked
     against.
2. **The engine's family packages at their pure interfaces:**
   - `recognise(read artefacts) → candidates + judgement requests`, on real drawings only (the
     real-drawing check);
   - `measure(facts, Rule Set version) → Measurement Lines`, on synthetic facts, with a QS's hand
     arithmetic written into the test for each rule.
3. **Checks** as pure functions over (read artefacts, confirmed state). Each has a firing and a
   passing case. The Sample Project's planted contradictions are the regression set (ADR 0004).

**The tests M2 must have:**
- **The Excel formula test** (`cloud`).
  - It generates the workbook for a fixture project that contains every kind of line: deduction
    rows, rebar by ratio and from the drawing, Lump Sums, a Provisional Sum, an allowance, covered
    labour, every layer and both taxes.
  - LibreOffice headless, an evaluator independent of the writer, recalculates it.
  - It reads back the computed values and asserts, **to the paisa, cell by cell,** that every
    amount, subtotal, layer, total, working rate and Material Schedule cell equals the API figure.
  - It also asserts that every amount, subtotal, layer and total cell is a formula, not a value;
    that no cell is `#REF!` or `#VALUE!`; and that changing one Market Price cell and recalculating
    moves exactly the dependent cells.
  - Real Windows Excel is checked by the owner in the walk: the test cannot prove it.
- **The shared format table test** (`cloud`): screen, Excel and PDF formatting of money,
  quantities, counts, lengths, levels and "—" against one table of expected strings, including
  negative lakh values.
- **Comparison invariants** (`cloud`, property-based):
  - for generated pairs of states, quantity effect + price effect = the change, per item and in
    total;
  - new and removed items, the "measured replaces allowance" rows, Lump Sum scope changes and Billing
    Unit changes all keep it;
  - q0 from an Issued Estimate equals a recomputation at its pins.
- **Issued immutability** (`cloud`):
  - after issuing, change a Market Price, a Rate Analysis, a rule (and accept the re-measure) and a
    Confirmation;
  - the issued figures and a re-export of the issue are unchanged (identical except the export's
    own timestamp);
  - the Market Price set is frozen and a new working set exists.
- **Rule editing** (`cloud`):
  - publishing a parameter edit moves no figure;
  - the preview shows only a quantity effect on the touched items;
  - accepting moves the pin;
  - Traces name the new version.
- **Rounding** (`cloud`): quantity × rate = amount on every row of the M2 fixture (extends M1's
  test).
- **The share link** (`cloud`):
  - it opens with no session;
  - it returns 404 when expired or revoked;
  - no ৳ and no anchor field appear in any payload;
  - a token of tenant A never reads tenant B (the RLS test);
  - it is read-only under every verb.
- **The assistant** (`cloud`):
  - on recorded Jev answers (ADR 0013), every figure in an answer equals the corresponding API
    figure;
  - low confidence and Banglish produce chips;
  - Jev unavailable produces empty chips.

  Routing accuracy is measured live, `local`, on the real-Ask spot-check set.
- **3D geometry** (`cloud`): the browser's geometry for walls with openings, stairs and tanks
  against the server's per-element volume and bounds (ADR 0022).
- **Showcase hygiene** (`cloud`): the "never show" patterns across the showcase fixture's API
  responses, Excel and PDF text. The same test runs `local` on the real showcase project before the
  walk.
- **The real-drawing check** (`local`, ADR 0030): every engine PR runs on the Sample Project and
  the Edison set and posts n / N per step. M2 adds the owner-confirmed expected N for footings,
  stair flights, tanks, openings (by type), rooms and lintels, all counted by a route independent of
  the reader. Walls are judged by the room-enclosure Check and by quantity, not counted: how a wall
  run is split is a convention, not a fact. A `local` reading ticket stops when its n / N stops
  improving.
- **The design gate:** every UI PR is walked by `ux-critic` at 1440 × 900 and 1280 × 800, and at
  390 × 844 for the Project Summary and the share link, with screenshots and Lighthouse.

**What `cloud` tests prove, and what they do not.** They prove the arithmetic, the invariants, the
formats, immutability, the API contracts, tenancy, the Excel formulas under an independent
evaluator, the PDF's text and the geometry's agreement between browser and server. They prove
nothing about reading drawings, about agreement with a Hand Takeoff, about real Excel on Windows or
about a phone's frame rate. Only the `local` real-drawing check and the owner's walk prove those.

**Prior art:** M1's quantity × rate test, its junction conservation Check, its Trace-resolution test
after a re-read, and its real-drawing check. The Summary and BOQ grid prototypes (screens.md) set
the reconciliation and performance bars.

## Out of Scope
- Rebar from the drawing for beams and slabs (M3), and for any family M2 adds; all of these are by
  ratio.
- Revisions:
  - reading a re-issued Drawing Set;
  - matching Elements across Revisions;
  - the Revision Comparison of a Revision;
  - "awaiting Confirmation" flags (M4).
- Vector-PDF Drawing Sets (M4).
- PWD's added rates per floor and per metre of height; the Benchmark is the base rate only.
- New Measurement Rules or formulas written by a Developer; editing is parameters and shipped
  alternatives only.
- MEP read or modelled; measured site works (a boundary wall or a drain measured from a drawing).
- Consumption ranges derived automatically from a Developer's past projects.
- Money, sheets or Traces on the share link.
- Assistant questions outside the fixed catalogue; generated prose; an LLM (Level 3).
- Bangla screens or documents; Bangla-script input is accepted, and answers are in English
  (ADR 0016).
- Per-diameter rebar prices; per-project Rate Analysis alternatives; multi-building projects
  (docs/data-model.md §7).
- A buyers' instalment plan; cast stages marked done; construction baselines.
- Importing a Developer's price list or BOQ from Excel.
- Furniture, interior fit-out and loose fittings beyond the Developer's Specification.
- Billing Developers for Vextrus itself.
- Beta operations (M5).

## Further Notes
- **What this spec assumes of M1.** It assumes M1 delivers:
  - steps 1–4, 6–8 and piles and pile caps;
  - rebar by ratio, and column rebar from the drawing;
  - Rate Analyses on editable Market Prices, and Labour Contracts with the labour-source Check;
  - allowances per step held as consumption per sft;
  - the Priced BOQ grid, with its per-floor item layout and the view by Trade;
  - the Material Schedule;
  - the Takeoff screen, the frame in 3D, the Check catalogue and Coverage;
  - the Jev client with its cache and override log;
  - telemetry per step;
  - an export the blind scorer reads.

  If M1's spec leaves out grade beams, slab on grade, Labour Contracts, the per-floor layout or the
  scoring export, M2's plan takes them on. This spec needs reconciling with the M0 and M1 specs being
  written in parallel before the plan is drafted.
- **Owner inputs** (values, not decisions; none has a primary source):
  - the AIT rate, from NBR;
  - the default ranges for cement bags, bricks and concrete cft per sft of Gross Floor Area;
  - the MEP and site template's lines and ৳/sft ranges;
  - the starter Developer's Specification;
  - the defaults for preliminaries, site overheads and contingency (PWD AnxB allows up to 8 % price
    and 2 % physical contingency);
  - verification of the extended library, including whether doors, windows, paint and
    waterproofing are supply-and-fix in Dhaka practice;
  - the minimum floor area below which a floor's ৳/sft shows "—".
- **The risks, most severe first:**
  1. Wall recall is the critical path. It has only been judged by eye, and it failed outright on a
     sparse ground floor without a layer map.
  2. Held-out Sets and Hand Takeoffs do not exist yet, and M2 cannot close without two of each.
  3. Rounding at the half-paisa in Excel against Python's exact Decimal may differ in rare ties. The
     formula test finds them; the fix is to carry dimension cells at full precision.
  4. Excel's two-condition custom formats make negative lakh values awkward.
  5. 3D on a phone has no budget yet. The plan proposes one (for example, first render ≤ 4 s on
     10 Mbps and ≥ 30 fps on a mid-range Android) and measures it.
  6. Banglish is Jev's measured weak spot (docs/research/jev-system-one.md §2.3). M2 notes it and
     does not fix it.
- **New terms** that may need `CONTEXT.md`: none yet. "Consumption checks" stays a kind of Check
  (the sanity range). "Measured replaces allowance" is a comparison row, not a term.

## Open questions for the owner
Asked one at a time, recommendation first.
1. **Tolerances for the item lines ADR 0005 does not name.**
   - Recommendation: earthwork and soling ±3 %; blinding ±1 % (as concrete); doors, windows and
     frames ±3 %; roof treatment ±5 % (as finishes).
   - Also: counts exact for footings, stair flights, tanks and lintels.
   - Also: zone ৳ totals exclude rebar by ratio on both sides.
   - Why: each follows the class nearest in how it is measured, and the finish line cannot be
     scored without them.
2. **The tax layer's base.**
   - Recommendation: VAT and AIT are both added cost, as a % of the Labour Contract and
     Material-and-Labour Contract amounts.
   - Why ask: AIT withheld at source is normally not an added cost unless the contractor's rate is
     net of tax, and the choice moves the headline figure.
3. **The share link shows the Building Model only: no ৳, no sheets.**
   - Recommendation: yes.
   - Why: a link can be forwarded, and the sheets are the consultant's content. An MD who wants to
     share money sends the PDF.
4. **Rule Set editing is limited to parameters and shipped alternatives: no new rules.**
   - Recommendation: yes.
   - Why: every rule stays a tested pure function, and a Developer's odd habit becomes a new shipped
     alternative instead.
5. **The assistant's finish-line bar.**
   - Recommendation: every figure exact (by construction); on the real-Ask set, routing correct on
     at least 90 % of English and Bangla-script asks, with every wrong route caught by the chips;
     Banglish measured and reported, not gated.
6. **The timed Takeoff.**
   - Recommendation: on Held-out Set 2, by a QS who did not build the product. Time, Confirmations
     and Questions per step are reported, not gated, and the owner judges the price with them after
     M2 (ADR 0033).
