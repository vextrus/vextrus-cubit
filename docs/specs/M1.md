## What is not yet known (read first)

- **No part of M1's reading has been measured on the families it adds, except columns and beams.**
  The session-10 prototype and the Sample Project first read never attempted piles, pile caps, shear
  walls, the lift core or slab-edge members, and read slab panel geometry at 0
  (docs/research/sample-project-first-read.md). Slabs and slab-edge members are M1's riskiest reading.
- **Rules fitted to one office transferred at 0 %** to the next (the same file: 17 fittings to run).
  M1 therefore closes only on a Held-out Set scored blind, not on the Development Sets.
- **Jev on real drawings:** storey ranges were right 19 / 38 in the plan review, so storey ranges and
  grids are code; every other node's accuracy here is from invented cases (docs/research/jev-system-one.md §2).
- **A QS's time per step has never been measured.** The prototype's 25 keystrokes for 58 columns is
  keystrokes, not reading time (docs/design/screens.md).
- **Owner inputs M1 cannot proceed without** are listed in Further Notes: the default allowance
  consumptions per Takeoff Step, the default diameter splits for rebar by ratio, the Hand Takeoffs and
  the Held-out Set.

## Problem Statement

A Dhaka Developer's QS gets a consultant's 2D structural drawings and must turn them into the frame's
quantities and money: piles, pile caps, columns, shear walls, the lift core, beams and slabs, with
their concrete, formwork and rebar. Today that is weeks of measurement sheets in Excel, read sheet by
sheet, where one element is measured on three sheets and a wrong schedule cell passes silently. The
MD waits for the whole takeoff before seeing any figure, and when the figure comes nobody can show
where a number was read. Rebar is either guessed from a thumb rule or counted bar by bar by hand.

After M0 the QS can upload the Drawing Set and see every sheet split, named and legible, but nothing
is measured and nothing is priced. Vextrus Cubit failed exactly here: it measured almost nothing,
refused any line missing one fact, and had no money at all (docs/postmortem.md, causes 2 and 4).

## Solution

The QS runs the Takeoff Steps that build the frame, in building order: sheets; general notes and
specification; storeys and levels; grid; piles and pile caps; columns, shear walls and the lift core;
beams; slabs with their slab-edge members. In each step the machine proposes every element it read,
with its Trace; the QS confirms the agreeing ones in bulk and the exceptions one by one; a fact the
machine could not read, or a Check that fired, becomes one Question at the point of need, answered
once for every element it unblocks. The frame grows in 3D after each Confirmation, coloured by
status.

From the first Confirmation the Priced BOQ shows a whole-building figure: every Takeoff Step not yet
confirmed carries a marked allowance, held as consumption per sft of Gross Floor Area and priced at
the Developer's Market Prices; each confirmed step replaces its allowance with its measured figure.
The frame's concrete, formwork and rebar are measured by the pinned Rule Set version (a pure
function of the confirmed facts), priced by Rate Analyses on the Developer's editable Market Prices,
with Labour Contracts as their own lines. Column rebar is read from the drawing; the rest is by
Rebar Ratio with an "assumed" diameter split, and every rebar figure shows its Rebar Basis. The
Material Schedule shows what to buy by Construction Stage and floor, in quantities and ৳. Every
figure opens its Trace.

M1 is done when the owner walks it on the Development Sets and on one Held-out Set, the figures agree
with the Hand Takeoff within tolerance, scored blind, and a QS who did not build Vextrus has run a
timed Takeoff.

## User Stories

### Starting the Takeoff (QS)
1. As a QS, I want the Takeoff to open on a step rail showing the fourteen Takeoff Steps in building

   I am and what is still an allowance.
2. As a QS, I want to type the Gross Floor Area from the architect's area statement when I start, so
   that the whole-building figure appears from my first Confirmation.
3. As a QS, I want to see which Rule Set version my project is measured by, by name and number, so
   that I know the rules behind every figure.
4. As a QS, I want to read every Measurement Rule my project uses in words, with its source (IS 1200,
   PWD or practice), so that I can settle an argument about a figure by pointing at its rule.
5. As a QS, I want each Takeoff Step to open only when the steps it depends on are confirmed (storeys
   and grid before any member; columns before beams; beams before slabs), so that no element is
   placed on facts I have not accepted.
6. As a QS, I want the layer-to-member map for a consultant confirmed once and remembered for that
   consultant's next set, so that I am not asked the same thing on every project.

### General notes and specification (step 2)
7. As a QS, I want each general-notes fact (concrete strength or Mix per element class, rebar grade,

## Implementation Decisions

### What M1 takes from M0
M1 assumes M0 delivered, and the plan reconciles any difference with `docs/specs/M0.md`:
- tenancy with row-level security, auth, memberships (QS, MD, Vextrus Engineer by invitation);
- Drawing Sets, Revisions, Sheets, Sheet Revisions, Drawing Set States and Views with confirmed
  scale; the read job (both decoders, quarantine); read artefacts per sheet at a reader version;
- the Trace anchor type and its resolve service; the sheet viewer on the engine's buffers, able to
  highlight an entity by its insert-handle chain;
- Coverage per view; the confirmed sheet list (Takeoff Step 1, whose Confirmation is "the first
  Confirmation");
- the platform's Jev client with its answer cache and fallback; jobs, events, storage; the
  formatters per figure kind (money and quantities in lakh, coordinates and dimensions never);
- the expected N values of the real-drawing check for the steps M0 and M1 need (ADR 0030).

### The module map in M1 (layers per docs/architecture.md; no module is added)
- **`engine`** gains `families/` with eleven packages (storey, grid_line, spec_note, pile, pile_cap,
  column, shear_wall, lift_core, beam, slab, slab_edge), the junction function and the pure measurer.
- **`building_model`** gains its tables (Family rows, Model Versions, Elements, Element States,
  confirmed rebar bars, Element Traces, View Placements).
- **`takeoff`** gains the Takeoff Step machinery, Proposals, Confirmations, Questions, the Check
  catalogue and runs, and Step Progress.
- **`measurement`** gains the Rule Set as data and the `measure` service.
- **`rates`** gains Resources, Market Price sets, Rate Analyses and Labour Contracts.
- **`boq`** gains the Priced BOQ computed on read, Cost Basis per Takeoff Step, the Material
  Schedule and the scoring export.
- **`projects`** gains the entered Gross Floor Area and the Display Units switch; `drawings` gains
  only what the reader-upgrade flow needs, if M0 did not build it.
- **Not touched in M1:** `summary`, `exports`, `assistant`, `revisions`.

### The family package contract (ADR 0031 §5)
Each package under `engine/families/<family>/` holds four pure functions and a manifest; a registry
is generated from the directory, so a family ticket only adds files.
- **manifest:** key, Takeoff Step, label, identity rule, milestone, the facts it carries (name and
  figure kind, for the generic Takeoff screen and inspector), the Checks it brings, the Measurement
  Rule codes its measure uses, the Jev nodes it calls, and how its N is counted. Family, Takeoff
  Step and Check rows are synced from manifests as data, so a family ticket adds no migration.
- **recognise(read artefacts of the views it may use, confirmed facts, project setup) → candidates +
  judgement requests.** Candidates carry values in drawing units, verbatim text and per-fact Trace
  anchors. Judgement requests are closed questions over candidates code found (ADR 0011); `takeoff`
  answers them through Jev or the QS.
- **check(read artefacts, confirmed state) → findings.** Never compared with the pipeline's own
  output (ADR 0027).
- **geometry(confirmed facts, owned solids) → parametric primitives** of three kinds only: an
  extruded polygon with holes, a cylinder, and an extruded polygon along a level offset. The browser
  knows only the primitives, so a family ticket never touches the viewer.
- **measure(confirmed facts, owned solids, Rule Set version) → Measurement Lines** (item code,
  storey, Nos × L × B × H or area, quantity in SI, rule codes, Rebar Basis, diameter, assumed flag).

### Takeoff Steps and their state
- **Step Progress:** not started → reading → in review → confirmed; a confirmed step may be
  reopened. A step is **confirmed** when every Proposal of its families is confirmed, rejected or
  excluded with a reason, and no Question blocks a Proposal, except rebar-only Questions, which
  leave the rebar figure "from the drawing + rules" (screens.md, Takeoff ruling 5).
- **Dependencies:** steps 2, 3 and 4 open after step 1; step 5 (piles, pile caps), 6 and later need
  3 and 4 confirmed; BOQ Items need step 2; step 7 needs 6; step 8 needs 7; pile caps need piles.
  A reopened step re-runs the Checks of every step that depends on it.
- **Questions** have a kind (missing, conflict, low confidence, Check, file misread, labour source)
  and a key hashed from the kind, the subject's identity and the evidence's content, so an answer is
  reused only while its evidence is unchanged. **Answering a Question confirms the Proposals it
  held**, in one Confirmation, and the card says so first. Question text is a code template chosen by
  the Check or reader that raised it; Jev never phrases a Question.
- **Pre-pick:** an answer is pre-picked only when two or more independent sources agree (drawn size,
  label text, schedule cell, per-sheet table), and the card names them.
- **Grouping:** Proposals group by mark within each Storey Band (or storey, for beams and slabs); the
  bulk Confirmation bar names what and why; the exceptions list sorts by effect on quantity, then ৳.
- **Edits and exclusions:** an edited fact becomes a new Proposal with source "QS typed" and a Trace
  of that kind; an exclusion keeps the element in N with its reason. Undo writes an "unconfirm"
  Confirmation and a new Model Version; history is append-only.
- **Coverage:** each view is used by a step, excluded with a reason, planned for a later step (a view
  carrying only M2 families, such as an architectural wall plan), or unaccounted. A view whose content
  a recogniser scores as carrying its family, but which the step did not use, raises a Question.
- **Consultant memory:** the layer-to-member map and the plan-versus-section precedence are confirmed
  once per consultant (identified from the title block) and stored per tenant; Jev is called only for
  names memory does not know.
- **The confirm service** is the only path into `building_model`: it converts drawing units to SI
  with exact factors (ADR 0008), copies Trace anchors, and calls `building_model`'s apply service,
  which opens and closes Element States and writes one Model Version. Every act writes one domain
  event.
- **Checks run** when a step's reading finishes and again at every Confirmation, across every step
  whose facts the Confirmation touched.

### Measuring (ADRs 0008, 0009, 0031 §1)
- **Measuring is pure:** `measurement.measure(project, facts version, Rule Set version)` reads the
  confirmed facts through `building_model`, calls the engine and returns Measurement Lines; it stores
  nothing. `takeoff` previews it on Proposals; `boq` caches it keyed by (facts version, Rule Set
  version, Display Units).
- **The Rule Set version M1 ships** is the Bangladeshi default as data in the Library: every rule of
  docs/research/qs-defaults.md §1.0–1.3 and P1–P2, with the practice parameters as set in
  docs/specs/bd-defaults.md; the BOQ Items and Billing Units for the whole starting library (so M2
  steps' allowances can name them); the Rebar Ratios; the diameter splits; the rounding rules; the
  Construction Stage rules; and the labour-measure item for casting area (CA1). It is published,
  immutable and pinned per project. Editing it is M2.
- **One junction function** assigns every concrete volume to one element by the Rule Set's joint rule
  (IS 1200 Pt 2 cl. 4.2.2 by default: pile stops at cut-off; column starts at the cap top and stops
  at the slab soffit; the slab runs over beams and columns; a beam runs between column faces below
  the slab). Both the measurer and the 3D geometry call it, and the conservation Check proves its
  result sums to the union.
- **Measured on what is confirmed:** a column confirmed before the slab above runs to the next level;
  when the slab is confirmed the joint passes to the slab. Totals are conserved; the item split moves
  and shows as a quantity change.
- **Heights and levels** come from step 3's storeys; a sunk panel sits at its confirmed local level.
- **Units:** drawings and Proposals stay in drawing units; the Building Model is SI; Measurement
  Lines are computed in SI and converted exactly into each Billing Unit; nothing that feeds a figure
  is a float.
- **Rounding** as the owner ruled (data-model §6, rulings 4 and 6): each Measurement Line to 2
  decimals in its Billing Unit and the item their sum; nos and bags whole; tons to 3 decimals; rebar
  lines to 2 decimals of a kg and the rebar item in whole kg; rates to the paisa; amount =
  ROUND(quantity × rate, 2); the Material Schedule rounds up.

### Rebar (ADR 0010)
- **Columns: from the drawing.** The column family reads each band's main bars and ties from the
  schedule (code parses candidate readings such as "8-20Ø + 4-16Ø" and "10Ø @ 6\" c/c"; Jev picks
  among them only where roles are ambiguous) and writes confirmed rebar bars in `building_model`:
  bar mark, role, diameter, count, cutting length, laps. Mass is length × standard mass per metre
  (R1).
- **Detailing rules** supply what the drawing is silent on, each a Measurement Rule of kind "rebar
  detailing" with its parameters, and each assumed length traced to its rule: laps (R3, 40 × d where
  unstated), tie hooks, anchorage into the pile cap, the top-storey anchorage, where laps fall, how
  ties are counted per spacing zone, and cover where the notes are silent. The proposed defaults are
  open question 4; the Hand Takeoff uses the same signed rules.
- **Everything else in M1 is by ratio:** piles, pile caps, shear walls, the lift core, beams, slabs
  and slab-edge members, as concrete volume × Rebar Ratio (R2), with the default diameter split per
  element type marked "assumed" in the Material Schedule. Per-Storey-Band ratio overrides wait for the
  editable Rule Set in M2.
- **Rebar Basis per Measurement Line**; a rebar item shows its share from the drawing.
- **Laps** are their own visible Measurement Lines and enter the Material Schedule; a Benchmark on
  net-of-lap kg waits for M2's Benchmark Rates.

### Money (ADRs 0002, 0006, 0028)
- **Rate Analyses:** the starting library of docs/specs/bd-defaults.md (the 18 items of qs-defaults
  §2.3 plus ready-mix RCC including the pump), plus the piling items open question 2 settles. One
  Rate Analysis per item, shown per 100 cft or 100 sft; editable in place by the Developer, each edit
  an event. Working rate per Billing Unit = Σ materials × (1 + wastage) × Market Price + labour +
  plant, exact, rounded to the paisa.
- **Market Prices** live in dated sets; edits go into the working set; each price keeps its
  last-changed date. Nothing in M1 freezes a set (Issued Estimates are M2).
- **A Resource without a Market Price** leaves its items unpriced and shown "rate not entered",
  never ৳0. The Library ships no labour rates (bd-defaults).
- **Labour Contracts** belong to the Developer, with a per-project override of rate or scope; each
  has its own labour-measure BOQ Item (casting area by CA1: each slab's area over beams and columns,
  per floor), its own line at the end of the BOQ Section whose items it covers, and a list of covered
  items whose own labour lines drop out.
- **BOQ Items** come from the Rule Set: the mix decides the item (from step 2 through the Developer's
  strength-to-item choice), the element class groups it, and numbering (section.group.item) is
  derived, never stored. In M1, Sub-structure holds piles, pile caps and columns from the cap top to
  the plinth (a Rule Set section rule); Super-structure holds columns, shear walls and the lift core,
  beams, slabs and slab-edge members.
- **Cost Basis per Takeoff Step** (ADR 0002 history, 26 Sep; screens.md, Summary rulings 4 and 7):
  - each step holds an allowance as consumption per sft of Gross Floor Area: a list of (BOQ Item,
    quantity per sft), or ৳ per sft where the step's items are Lump Sums (step 14);
  - its ৳ is that consumption × Gross Floor Area, priced at the current Market Prices;
  - it shows as one allowance line in the step's BOQ Section, never split, hatched and worded;
  - its source is Vextrus's default (set by the owner) or typed by the QS (recorded);
  - confirming the step replaces the allowance whole with the step's measured figure; reopening a
    step keeps it measured;
  - before then, "measured so far: ৳X of an allowance of ৳Y" shows beside it, and its measured
    items show beneath in a muted group that is not counted;
  - step 5 carries two allowance parts in M1 (open question 1).
- **The whole-building figure** is the direct cost: measured + allowance, with the measured share as
  one bar and cost per sft of Gross Floor Area. The Estimate's layers are M2. It needs a Gross Floor
  Area: until one exists the figure shows "—" with the action to enter it (open question 5).
- **The Material Schedule** is Σ item quantity × Rate Analysis line × (1 + wastage), by Resource,
  storey and Construction Stage, gross, rounded up, in quantities and ৳; Material-and-Labour
  materials are excluded; rebar is split by diameter (read, or "assumed"). Stage rules are Rule Set
  data: piles to piling; pile caps and columns up to the plinth to substructure; each column to the
  casting of the slab above it; beams, slabs and slab-edge members to their own slab casting.
  Whether allowances appear in it is open question 6. Each Resource has an editable procurement lead
  time shown against its first stage.
- **Nothing live is stored:** the Priced BOQ, the Material Schedule and the whole-building figure are
  computed on read from the Measurement Line cache and the current prices.

  geometry.
- Proposals draw from their candidate geometry in `takeoff` (dashed cyan at 45 %); confirmed elements
  are concrete grey; elements with an open Question amber, pulsing once on arrival; elements of steps
  not yet taken off are absent.
- M1 ships orbit, fit, storey isolate, pick, and selection shared with the inspector, the sheet and the

- Budgets (ADR 0022) are measured on the Edison frame on the reference laptop: model on screen ≤ 1.5 s

## Testing Decisions

- **A good test states behaviour at the highest seam that shows it,** never an implementation
  detail. Synthetic fixtures prove mechanics only; they are never offered as proof of reading.
- **Seam 1, the whole chain (highest; the one that matters most):** through `takeoff`'s public
  services and HTTP API, a synthetic project's Proposals are confirmed, and the Priced BOQ, the
  Material Schedule and the whole-building figure are read back through `boq`'s API. It proves the
  typed chain element → Measurement Line → BOQ Item → rate → amount → material end to end: answering
  a Question confirms its elements; excluding keeps N; undo restores the previous Model Version's
  figures; a confirmed step replaces its allowance; a Market Price edit moves rates, amounts,
  allowances and materials and nothing else; a Labour Contract drops covered labour and the
  labour-source Check fires on a double cover; quantity × rate = amount on every row; switching
  Display Units re-bills; the Material Schedule rounds up.
- **Seam 2, the engine's pure functions:** the measurer and the junction function on synthetic
  frames, with property tests: owned volumes sum exactly to the union for random frames; unit
  conversions are exact; each rule (F1–F8, FW1–FW6, P1–P2, R1–R3 and the detailing rules) has a
  worked example taken from its words, which the owner can read.
- **Seam 3, the family contract:** each family's recognise and check on small synthetic views for
  mechanics (`cloud`), and on the real sets through the real-drawing check (`local`).
- **The real-drawing check (ADR 0030)** runs on every PR touching `engine` or the reading modules:
  per Development Set, n / N per Takeoff Step against the owner-confirmed expected N values, the
  change against the last merged run element by element, and the count of changed attributes per
  family. Aggregates only; a `local` reading ticket stops when its n / N stops improving.
- **Reader upgrade:** a `cloud` test on a synthetic set asserts zero Proposals, zero Element States,
  zero Questions, an unchanged facts version and every new anchor resolving; the same assertions run
  `local` on both Development Sets.
- **3D:** a CI test compares the browser's geometry with the server's per-element volume and bounds
  (ADR 0022); budgets are measured on the reference laptop at the walk.
- **Jev:** tests use recorded answers; live calls run only in tests marked live, never with the cloud
  key on real text; each node's spot check runs `local`.
- **UI:** the design gate (docs/sdlc.md) on every UI PR at 1440×900 and 1280×800 against a seeded
  synthetic project; a browser test drives the Takeoff by keyboard (confirm a band, answer a
  Question, exclude, undo) and asserts the counts and figures shown. The browser test tool is
  chosen in the plan.
- **Formatters:** screen figures are checked against the one shared table of expected strings.
- **What `cloud` tests prove:** the mechanics, the arithmetic and the contracts, on synthetic data.
  **What `local` tests prove:** no regression on the Development Sets. **What proves reading:** only
  the Held-out Set, scored blind at the walk.
- **Prior art:** none in the product yet; M0's tests (the read job, the row-level security policy
  test) are the first, and M1's follow their layout.

## Further Notes

### Why the M1 / M2 boundary falls where it does
1. **Piles and pile caps are M1; the rest of step 5 is M2** (ADRs 0002, 0007 as amended). Grade beams
   look like beams, but they sit on their own layout with slab-on-grade and earthwork decisions
   that belong together; pulling them in would pull derived earthwork in.
2. **Pile boring and pile-head breaking are measured in M1,** though the milestone line says
   "concrete, formwork, rebar". Confirming piles replaces step 5's piles-and-caps allowance whole; if
   boring were not measured, the whole-building figure would drop by the piling contract's value on
   Confirmation. They are pure functions of pile facts M1 confirms anyway.
3. **The Labour-source Check is M1,** though not in the task's list, because Labour Contracts are M1
   and ADR 0006 makes the Check the condition for them.
4. **Rate Analysis editing is M1;** Rule Set editing is M2. A QS who may not type over a rate
   (screens.md, grid ruling 3) needs a way to correct a coefficient on day one; a rule edit needs
   the re-measure preview that comes with Issued Estimates and Comparisons.
5. **The whole-building figure lives on top of the Priced BOQ in M1,** readable by the MD on desktop;
   the Project Summary and its phone layout are M2, as the milestones table says.
6. **Display Units, the per-floor item layout and procurement lead times are M1:** each is small, and
   each is part of the BOQ or Material Schedule M1 delivers (ADRs 0008, 0002; grid ruling 7).
7. **The stair and lift tower's frame is M1** (its columns, core, beams and slabs are steps 6–8); the
   stair itself, the lift pit, parapets and roof treatment are M2.
8. **All of step 8 is M1,** slab-edge members included, because a step's allowance is replaced whole
   on its Confirmation: a partial step 8 would drop class (d) money.

### Mismatches found in the documents (for a fix, not a decision)
- **ADR 0002's first paragraph and docs/milestones.md's M1 line still say allowances are per
  Trade** ("every Trade carries a Cost Basis", "every unmeasured trade carries a marked allowance");
  the ruling of 26 Sep in ADR 0002's own history, CONTEXT.md and screens.md say per Takeoff Step. This
  spec follows the later ruling.
- **docs/data-model.md holds Cost Basis per Trade** (`TradeBasis`); M1 needs it per Takeoff Step with
  consumption lines.
- **docs/data-model.md still names the rebar tables and fields with the old word** (`RodBar`,
  `RodRatio`, `rod_basis`, `ROD-500W`); the build uses Rebar names.
- **ADR 0026's scorer returns "aggregates per Element Family";** M1's finish line needs aggregates per
  BOQ Item line per zone and per zone ৳ as well. Still aggregates, but the scorer must be extended.

## Finish line

### The owner's walk, step by step
Preconditions: M0 has passed; the M1 Rule Set version is signed; the Hand Takeoffs and the Held-out

   mark arrives as a local-level Question; every plan view is placed on its storeys.

   the rest of step 5 stays an allowance.
6. Step 6: columns by Storey Band, shear walls and the lift core are confirmed in bulk with the
   exceptions answered; column rebar shows "from the drawing" or "from the drawing + rules" with its
   lap lines; no column rebar line is by ratio.
7. Steps 7 and 8: beams, slabs and slab-edge members are confirmed; the frame is whole in 3D, coloured
   by status, a storey isolated, one element picked and selected in the inspector, sheet and grid.
8. Every step shows n / N with N from the drawing; Coverage has no unaccounted view.
9. In the Priced BOQ, any figure the owner picks opens its Trace: the Measurement Line, its rules and
   the sheet at the entity, or the Question that supplied it. Quantity × rate = amount on every row.
   The metric switch re-bills and switches back.
10. The owner changes the rebar Market Price: rates, amounts, the unmeasured steps' allowances and the
    Material Schedule's ৳ move; quantities do not.
11. A Labour Contract per sft of casting area covering casting, shuttering and rebar binding is set
    up: it appears as its own line, the covered items drop their labour, and covering one item twice
    raises a labour-source Question.
12. The Material Schedule shows piling, substructure and each slab casting by floor, in quantities and
    ৳, rebar by diameter (read for columns, "assumed" elsewhere); a cell opens to its BOQ Items.
13. The MD signs in and sees the whole-building figure, the measured share and the steps still on
    allowance, read-only.
14. A reader upgrade re-read gives zero changes, and every Trace still opens.
15. The scoring export is produced, and the owner runs the blind scorer: every row of the table below
    passes.

## Open questions for the owner (one at a time, recommendation first)

1. **Step 5's allowance in M1.** Recommendation: hold it in two parts, "piles and pile caps"
   (replaced when M1's part of step 5 is confirmed) and "rest of foundations and substructure"
   (allowance until M2), both in Sub-structure. Otherwise confirming piles either wipes out the money
   for grade beams and earthwork or keeps an allowance over measured piles. This refines "replaced
   whole when the step is confirmed" for the one step M1 splits.
2. **How piling is priced.** Recommendation: pile concrete by its own Rate Analysis (materials, with
   the over-cast as a visible line), boring measured in rft and pile-head breaking per pile, both
   priced by a default Labour Contract "piling per rft" whose rate the Developer enters. Dhaka
   Developers usually let piling to a piling contractor; you know whether it is labour-only or
   material-and-labour.
3. **Counting beams and slab panels for "counts exact".** Recommendation: a beam is one span between
   supports (matching rule F3 and identity by axis segment); a slab panel is one region bounded by
   beams or edges with one thickness and level. Written into the Rule Set, so the Hand Takeoff counts
   the same way.
4. **Column rebar detailing defaults** (with R3's 40 × d laps), from practice, unverified, each a
   parameter: tie hooks 135° with 6 × d extension, at least 75 mm (the ACI 318 seismic hook, clause
   to verify); one lap per bar per storey, above the slab; column bars anchored into the pile cap to
   its bottom mat with a 12 × d bend; top-storey bars to the top of the roof slab less cover with a
   12 × d bend; ties counted per stated spacing zone as length ÷ spacing + 1, continuing through the
   beam depth; no bend deductions; cover 40 mm where the notes are silent. Recommendation: accept, or
   correct any line, before the Hand Takeoff of columns.
5. **The Gross Floor Area in M1.** Recommendation: use the QS-entered figure from the area statement
   until walls are confirmed in M2, with the area measured from confirmed slabs shown beside it and
   flagged if the two differ by more than 5 %. The Gross Floor Area is defined to the outside of the
   walls, which M1 does not measure.
6. **Allowance materials in the Material Schedule.** Recommendation: yes, marked "allowance" and
   hatched, in the stage the step feeds and in one "not yet by floor" column until the storeys'
   areas are known. Cement and rebar totals are then whole-building from the first Confirmation, like
   the ৳.
7. **The starter Market Price set.** Recommendation: ship PWD SoR 2022 (2nd Revised) material input
   prices as the starter set, cited and dated, labelled "PWD prices, replace with yours"; ship no
   labour rates, so labour shows "rate not entered" until the Developer enters its Labour Contracts.
   The alternative is an empty set, with no figure until the Vextrus Engineer enters prices.
8. **Rebar from the drawing for shear walls and the lift core** is in no milestone (ADR 0010 names
   columns, beams and slabs). Recommendation: add it to M3 after slabs, so ratio rebar is not the
   permanent answer for the core, as your condition requires.
9. **A pass mark for the timed Takeoff.** Recommendation: none in M1; record time, Confirmations and
   Questions per step, and set it beside the outside QS's own estimate of a manual frame takeoff for
   the same set. Setting a mark before the first measurement would be a guess.
10. **How a stated concrete strength becomes a BOQ Item.** Recommendation: the Developer keeps a table
    of strength to BOQ Item (a site Mix such as 1:1.5:3, or ready-mix of a grade), pre-picked in step
    2 per element class and remembered for its next project, because consultants' notes state f′c
    while the items and Rate Analyses are by Mix.

### The owner's rulings (26 Sep 2026): "Agree with all; piling is both labour-only and material-and-labour, detailing defaults fine"
All ten recommendations above are accepted, with question 2 settled as: **piling is let both ways in
Dhaka**, so the Developer chooses per project: a labour-only piling Labour Contract (per rft of boring
and per pile for head breaking, with pile concrete and rebar through their Rate Analyses into the
Material Schedule), or a piling Material-and-Labour Contract (one rate per rft or per pile, its
materials kept out of the Material Schedule). The column rebar detailing defaults of question 4 are
accepted as written, each a Rule Set parameter.
